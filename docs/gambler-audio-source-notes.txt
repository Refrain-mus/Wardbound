PALE GAMBLER — AUDIO SOURCE NOTES

This pass separates cast, card/table contact, heavy impact, and speech-stinger roles so the fight does not read as one continuous whoosh bank.

Current integrated assets in assets/wardbound/sounds/gambler/ are self-contained Wardbound derivatives/reprocesses of the project's existing audio bank, including:
- heavy_hit_1.ogg / heavy_hit_2.ogg / heavy_hit_3.ogg
- speech_1.ogg / speech_2.ogg / speech_3.ogg
- cross_cast.ogg / cross_impact.ogg
- chip_cast.ogg / chip_impact.ogg
- pot_cast.ogg / pot_impact.ogg

CC0 packs researched as safe future source material / replacement layers:

1) BMacZero — Playing Card Sounds — CC0
   https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds
   Useful files: shuffle, contact1/contact2, cut.

2) Kenney — Impact Sounds — CC0
   https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
   130 impact/foley files suitable for table, body and heavy-hit layers.

3) psychhead_ — Horror Hit Soundpack 1 — CC0
   https://opengameart.org/content/horror-hit-soundpack-1
   55 horror impact WAVs suitable for phase breaks and lethal attacks.

4) qubodup — Ghost Monster Voice Moaning & Growling — CC0
   https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling
   Useful as non-verbal occult vocal texture beneath Gambler dialogue stingers.

5) Sorth — Ghost Voice Pack — CC0
   https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-voice-pack
   Contains voice lines, hurt noises and evil laughter; uploader confirms the recorded voice is their own in the source comments.

Those researched external packs are not redistributed in this repository; only source/licensing references are recorded here. The sound-event architecture is already split into the roles above so CC0 replacements can be dropped in without touching combat code.

Additional vetted CC0 source packs:

7) BMacZero — Mechanical Sounds — CC0
