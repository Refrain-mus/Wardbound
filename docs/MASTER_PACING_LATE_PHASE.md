# Master pacing — late phase

The Ashen Curator, Mourning Notary and Pale Gambler are peer Masters. They are not a sequential tutorial ladder.

- Shared field-card dealer gate: **120 opened field cards**.
- All three become eligible at the same moment and use the normal anti-streak weighting from that point onward.
- Shared identity threshold: **160 opened field cards**, plus at least **3 real audiences with that specific Master**.
- Before 120, loose cards are presented as **Unclaimed Hand** and cannot advance Master relation, grudge, audience, story, rivalry, signature-card injection or Master dialogue.
- Card-lineage resonance can accumulate silently before the Master phase, so early card decisions still matter later, but Master names are not emitted by lineage milestone messages before the identity is known.
- Ledger entries that explicitly advertise late Master systems (Occult Chains and Ash Archive) stay hidden until the shared Master phase begins.
- MasterStory has a defensive shared-gate check, preventing unrelated systems from accidentally starting Master chapters early.

Design intent: a new player should initially understand Wardbound as a structure-ward/minigame and card-progression mod. The Master layer should feel like a later discovery rather than the mod immediately advertising its endgame hierarchy.
