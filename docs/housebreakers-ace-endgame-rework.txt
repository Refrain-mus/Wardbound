HOUSEBREAKER'S ACE — END-ENDGAME REWARD REWORK

The old disposable clauses (+1 life next ward / +35% loot next ward with -1 life) were intentionally removed as the primary reward.
The Pale Gambler is an end-endgame Master; the Ace now steals permanent rules from the House.

LAW I — THE HOUSE OWES YOU
Permanent on every ordinary ward:
- +1 starting life
- +4 seconds to every minigame
- first meaningful error in each minigame is pardoned
- +25% ward loot

LAW II — THE WORLD IS ANOTHER TABLE
Permanent world law:
- +15% outgoing damage to hostiles
- 12% incoming damage reduction (does not blunt invulnerability-bypassing kill/void damage)
- +10% movement speed
- +4 max hearts
- +1.5 block reach
- +1 entity reach
- +2 Luck
- Wardbound Master-tagged bosses take an additional +25% damage

Each law can be signed exactly once. First signature leaves the Ace intact; second consumes it.
The master-boss damage clause uses data/wardbound/tags/entity_types/master_bosses.json so future Masters can opt into the same reward cleanly.
