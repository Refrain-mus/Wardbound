# Wardbound 1.0 — Maestro / Beyond-the-Three Progression

## Cosmology

Gambler, Curator and Notary remain the three Masters known to Silas and to the existing card ecology. They do not secretly serve Maestro and Maestro is not their superior in an organization. The important reveal is epistemic: all four existed beyond one another's maps.

Silas believes the three known Masters exhaust the meaningful threat record. Defeating all three produces **Score Beyond the Margin**, a fourth signature whose hand matches none of them. This is the first proof that the known Master taxonomy was local, not cosmic.

Maestro was human. His lifespan outgrew ordinary chronology, and with time he learned to hear stable relationships between probability, causality and dimensional boundaries as musical structure. He eventually learned to cross realms by reproducing those structures. This did not make him omniscient: it made him exceptionally old, mobile, and able to conduct local fate.

He has witnessed planets and civilizations end, including extinctions caused by entities vastly beyond the known Masters. He processes these events almost exclusively as art. Awe, grief, religion and terror have collapsed into composition for him. His reverence is therefore expressed as performance: **“To the sublimity that bestowed art, I return these notes.”**

He does not know Gambler, Curator or Notary as colleagues. Their world is simply one of many places he could have crossed. He does, however, recognize traces of the Sleeper/Cthulhu from manifestations encountered across worlds. He has not stood before the complete body.

## Progression

1. Defeat Pale Gambler.
2. Defeat Ashen Curator.
3. Defeat Mourning Notary.
4. The third conclusion reveals **Score Beyond the Margin**. Old saves with all three defeated receive it on login.
5. Right-click the Score to enter the private Maestro stage.
6. Defeat Maestro.
7. Reward includes **Canticle for the Sleeper** in addition to the material reward.
8. The Canticle can only be invoked while physically in **the End**. It opens the existing private Head-of-Cthulhu encounter.
9. Defeat the Head to receive **Abyssal Vestige**.
10. Future work: an End altar will require **Abyssal Vestige + Cthulhu Idol** to call the complete Cthulhu encounter. That final summon is intentionally not implemented in 1.0.

## Maestro combat

- Exact 0.8 x 2.0 block combat body.
- **11,000 authored vitality** through AttributeFix/native-health bridge.
- Higher armor, attack damage and knockback resistance than 39.7.x.
- Faster attack cadence.
- Stronger phase-scaled Apothic Attributes: armor pierce/shred, crit chance/damage, life steal, current-HP damage and dodge.
- Existing 45-animation GeckoLib performance rig is preserved.
- Combat remains solo-contract authoritative.

## Head lore

The Head now explicitly states during manifestation, phase changes and death that it is not the complete body. It describes the arena form as a survivable/proximal manifestation. The Head reward therefore points forward instead of pretending the final Cthulhu confrontation has already occurred.
