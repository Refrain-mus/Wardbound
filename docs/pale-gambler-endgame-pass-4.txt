WARD BOUND — PALE GAMBLER ENDGAME PASS 4

This pass turns the Gambler's late fight into a mechanical final exam rather than a larger random attack pool.

NEW HANDS
- Dead Man's Draw: four coffin cards are collected in sequence; phase 3 finishes with an opposite-card double collection.
- False Tell: the House deliberately presents false rays; only the final reveal identifies the real red cuts and teal bluffs.
- Suit Call: each participating player receives a personal Spade/Heart/Club/Diamond seal. Their own seal is highlighted privately.
- Royal Flush: five large floor cards form a hand; selected pairs are collected over multiple waves.
- The Last Deal: a forced six-stage phase-3 exam combining Dealer Cross, Split Pot, Double Down, Mirror Debt, Royal sanctuary and False Tell.

ENDGAME SYSTEMS
- House Read: consecutive clean hands progressively expose the Gambler and increase the punish multiplier.
- Final Tell: completing The Last Deal cleanly opens the largest vulnerability window.
- ALL IN: below 8% vitality the Gambler removes most recovery, increases outgoing pressure and uses a lethal endgame hand pool.

PRESENTATION
- New authored textures: dead_mans_draw, false_tell, four suit seals, royal_flush, last_deal, all_in, house_read, final_tell.
- New GeckoLib animations for all five new hands.
- Boss bar now displays the live hand, House Read level, Final Tell and ALL IN state.
- All 512 authoritative telegraph cues may now render; ornate floor geometry is no longer silently cut at 260.

ARM / HAND FIX
- The model faces -Z. Positive X arm pitch is therefore the real forward cast direction.
- Combat animations have been normalized to positive-X forward keyframes.
- Renderer runtime clamps the arm/hand chain into the forward hemisphere and biases shoulder/hand pivots toward the coat front.
- No non-idle Gambler arm/hand keyframe retains a negative-X attack pitch.

ROBUSTNESS
- Texture FX list has a hard 1280 cosmetic safety cap.
- GamblerTextureFx flushes only render types it owns instead of globally ending the entire client buffer batch.
- Master cue packet remains capped at 512 sender-side, while the Gambler renderer now consumes that complete cap.
