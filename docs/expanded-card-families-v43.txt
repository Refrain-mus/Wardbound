WARDBOUND — EXPANDED PROGRESSION CARD FAMILIES

IDs 180–229 are revision-capable world-play Wagers. Stable signatures last 4 minutes,
Revision I lasts 7 minutes, Revision II lasts 10 minutes, and Palimpsest copies are permanent.
The cards remain conditional so collecting many of them does not collapse into one flat stat stack.

MASTER LINEAGE
Each card has a hidden thematic affinity to the Ashen Curator, Mourning Notary, or Pale Gambler.
Signing, revising, and especially Palimpsesting affinity cards builds persistent Master resonance.
Cards offered by their matching Master also count more strongly. CardMaster.prefers() now recognizes
these affinities, so dealer relation/story and future boss encounters can use the same choices.

CardLineage.bossReadiness(data, player, master) is the explicit future-boss hook.
Witness Ledger's Master Threads detail exposes the accumulated resonance without making it a hard quest counter.

DEAL COMPOSITION
Ordinary hands deliberately cap expanded progression cards to one slot in two/three-card hands
and two slots in four-card hands, with no duplicate family inside the same hand. This prevents the
50 new cards from drowning the existing deck. Dealer-authored loose hands do not use that cap;
Master affinity is allowed to make those hands feel more specialized.
