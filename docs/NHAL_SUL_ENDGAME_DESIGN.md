# Nhal-Sûl // Ancient Smith acquisition

Nhal-Sûl is a registered, usable endgame relic with one intended survival acquisition route.

Current rules:
- `wardbound:nhal_sul` deals 40 total attack damage at 1.4 attack speed.
- It has no durability lifecycle and does not wear down or break.
- Unwritten Cut is immediately available once the player receives the blade: right-click to release the cut instantly, even into empty space. Solid blocks shorten the cut and a 12-second cooldown prevents spam.
- Unwritten Cut deals 70 raw damage, reaches 18 blocks, can strike up to 8 valid targets and has a 12 s cooldown.
- The former 800/900-point hidden bond gate and night/low-health/crouch awakening requirements are removed.
- The first successful Cut still marks the blade as awakened for presentation/lore reactions; this is not a gameplay gate.

Survival acquisition:
1. Defeat any two distinct Masters.
2. Once the player is alive, out of a Master encounter, out of combat and otherwise safe, the Ancient Forge notices the second fallen Master.
3. After a short safety delay, the player is taken to the Ancient Smith's private forge cell.
4. The Smith completes a second Nhal-Sûl and throws it to the player.
5. Picking it up completes the transaction and returns the player to their recorded entry location.

The Ancient Smith is the only survival source. Silas, the Worn Scabbard Ring, testimony fragments, Phase III dismissal, ordinary Master victory rewards, chests, drops and crafting do not grant Nhal-Sûl. Admin/creative commands remain available for development testing.

Failure safety:
- Logout, death, dimension escape, Smith unload or cinematic timeout aborts an acquisition without creating the sword.
- A loaded/restarted acquisition session enters recovery rather than replaying the reward.
- Completion is recorded only after the actual offer is claimed.
