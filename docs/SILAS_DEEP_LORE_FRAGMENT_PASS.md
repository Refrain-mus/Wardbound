# Silas Veyr — Deep Lore / Unresolved Testimonies Pass

## Core thesis

Silas Veyr does not survive because he is stronger than death. His death cannot complete. The Gambler, Curator and Notary left mutually incompatible claims over his ending: an unresolved outcome, a death whose final version cannot acquire exclusive archival authority, and a judgment whose witness line never closed. None of this was done out of mercy. The Masters do not need to personally care about Silas for the contradiction to persist.

Silas himself does not possess the complete explanation. He knows that he has crossed fatal thresholds more than once and that returning stopped feeling miraculous long ago. The player reconstructs the actual mechanism from physical testimony fragments and Witness Ledger observations.

## Progression stages

0. TRACE — any Master story reaches chapter III. Silas does not appear yet. Rare anonymous road fragments begin to exist.
1. WAYFARER — any Master reaches chapter IV. Silas can appear. He speaks about the road, Nhal-Sûl, old titles and ordinary loss. No Master names.
2. THREE COMPANIONS — any Master reaches chapter V. Sera and İlen become central. The player learns that success, not the first defeat, began the catastrophe.
3. THE HANDS BEHIND IT — any Master reaches chapter VI. Silas finally names Gambler, Curator and Notary and explains their roles through personal experience rather than encyclopedic exposition.
4. UNCONCLUDED — any Master reaches VII or the player defeats a first Master. Silas can speak about fear and repeated fatal thresholds. Late fragments reveal that his death is structurally unresolved.
5. WITNESS — two Masters defeated. Silas becomes terse. He is no longer a teacher or quest-giver; the player has moved beyond the point from which he can instruct them.

## Dialogue rules

- One line per interaction, presented in the dedicated Silas dialogue window.
- Each progression stage owns its own looping pool. No later-stage line can appear early.
- Sneak-right-click repeats the last line from the current stage.
- Stage V uses a smaller pool and a longer interaction cooldown.
- Silas never provides the complete immortality explanation himself.

## Physical fragment system

Fragments may appear rarely in successfully resolved Wardbound chests once their progression gate is legal. Eligible failed rolls build a small pity counter, so prolonged bad luck cannot permanently hide the story. A fragment is a persistent physical item. Right-clicking it opens a dedicated document screen and copies the discovery into Witness Ledger → UNRESOLVED TESTIMONIES.

Fragments:
1. The Innkeeper's Account — anonymous pre-encounter blood / sleeplessness trace.
2. Names on the Grip — learned by spending time close to Silas and observing Nhal-Sûl, not by chest loot.
3. A Physician's Refusal — anonymous pre-encounter fatality anomaly.
4. The Unclaimed Hand — stake accepted; outcome unresolved.
5. Archive Entry 44–V — death indexed; final state unavailable; record disagreement.
6. The Death Certificate Without a Death — complete death details, empty witness line.
7. Sera's Morning — deliberately ordinary human memory; no cosmic exposition.
8. The Champion's Return — external proof that Silas was buried and later returned.
9. Curator: Cross-Reference — no single ending retains exclusive authority.
10. A Margin in Silas' Hand — proof that fear came to replace the simpler hatred he once felt.
11. The Notary's Addendum — death acknowledged, conclusion denied, matter remains in force.
12. The Curator's Final Annotation — persistence is explicitly not interpreted as mercy.

## Unconcluded fatal sequence

A fatal hit does not call a completed death state. Silas enters a six-second UNCONCLUDED state:
- navigation and combat stop;
- body collapses / kneels rather than despawning;
- ambience and sound design thin into anchor/sculk/amethyst layers;
- reverse-portal motes mark the point at which the world fails to close the event;
- nearby players permanently receive OBSERVATION // HIS DEATH DID NOT COMPLETE;
- Silas returns at roughly 30% health and spends a recovery period in the weary state.

Nhal-Sûl never becomes death loot.

## Master relation

Gambler and Notary have no personal Silas reaction. Curator alone may acknowledge his record once the player has reached the stage at which the Masters have been named. This is archival interest and restrained respect, not concern or affection.
