# Silas Veyr // Phase III dismissal

Silas may answer the Worn Scabbard Ring during Master Phase I and II. When the encounter crosses into its final Phase III, the Master forcibly dismisses him. This is a dramatic encounter beat: the player must finish the final phase alone.

The dismissal record remains useful as Silas/Master lore, but **does not grant Nhal-Sûl and is not an acquisition prerequisite for the sword**.

The Worn Scabbard Ring remains exclusively Silas's summon item.
